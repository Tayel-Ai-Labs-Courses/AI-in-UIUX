# Timeline — 5 weeks

Dates are filled in per cohort. Duration is what matters.

| Week | Focus | Due at end of week | Points at stake |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Research, user choice, competitive teardown | `01-research/` | 15 |
| 2 | IA, flows, lo-fi wireframes | `02-wireframes/` | 15 |
| 3 | Visual direction, design system, first hi-fi screen | Draft of `03-ui/` | — |
| 4 | All hi-fi screens, all states, mobile | `03-ui/` complete | 20 + 15 |
| 5 | Prototype, walkthrough, handoff, final polish | `04-prototype/`, `05-handoff/` | 15 + 20 |

## Reviews

- **End of week 1 and week 2 — written feedback.** Push your work; feedback
  comes back on the repo. Act on it; the same note twice costs points.
- **Week 3 — live critique, 15 minutes each.** Bring your design system page
  and one hi-fi screen. Not optional.
- **Week 5 — final presentation, 10 minutes.** Walk the flow, then defend two
  decisions you expect to be challenged on.

## The realistic advice

Weeks 1 and 2 feel slow and unimportant. They are the ones that decide your
grade. Every student who struggles in week 4 struggles because they started
drawing pretty screens in week 1 without knowing who they were for.

Push to the repo **as you go**, not the night before. A commit history that
shows three days of work reads exactly like three days of work.
